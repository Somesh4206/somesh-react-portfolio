Case Study 03

DiagnoraX AI

Your Personal AI Health Companion

DiagnoraX AI is a Flutter/Dart mobile health companion using Firebase Auth/Firestore and Gemini 2.0 Flash with a Groq fallback. Verified: symptom checking with severity, prescription OCR, lab-report analysis, drug interactions, doctor suggestions, body analysis and reminders UI. Developed building on ML internship experience at Brainery Spot Technologies.

01 · Overview

Overview

  • Describe symptoms, scan prescriptions/reports, check interactions, get doctor suggestions, track reminders.
  • Routes everyday health chores through one AI service with shared severity/risk language.
  • For personal awareness and organisation — not diagnosis or treatment.
  • One GeminiService fronts text, vision and fallback paths; Firestore profiles; single dark theme.

02 · The Problem

The Problem

  • Everyday questions (concerning symptom? prescription content? report meaning?) lack one organised starting point.
  • Handwriting and jargon are hard to parse alone.
  • Medication lists grow without interaction visibility.

03 · The Solution

The Solution

  • Thirteen screens behind a 6-tab shell covering checker, scanner, analyser, interactions, doctors, body, reminders, dashboard, profile, auth.
  • Central service with typed models (SymptomAnalysis, ReportAnalysis, DoctorRecommendation) and shared SeverityBadge widgets.
  • Firebase Auth (email, phone OTP, Google) + Firestore profiles.

07 · Technical Implementation

Technical Implementation

  • Flutter/Dart, Material dark, 13 screens, setState.
  • No own backend — Firebase + direct AI HTTPS calls.
  • Image paths use base64 inline_data.
  • firebase_core/auth/firestore, google_sign_in, shared_preferences, dotenv.
  • image_picker, compression, url_launcher, permissions.

10 · Challenges & Decisions

Challenges & Decisions

  • No backend → direct AI calls with dotenv + setup banner → zero-server deploy.
  • Varied image inputs → picker + compression + typed vision outputs.
  • Honest scope → reminders as UI-only, no SOS (only disclaimer string).

11 · Result

Result

  • Symptom severity, prescription OCR, report scoring, interactions, doctor recs, body analysis, auth, profiles.
  • Limits: reminders UI-only; no SOS; no React/Express in this checkout.
  • Note: awareness aid, not a replacement for professional medical care.

DiagnoraX is a health-organisation and awareness aid, not a replacement for professional medical care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
